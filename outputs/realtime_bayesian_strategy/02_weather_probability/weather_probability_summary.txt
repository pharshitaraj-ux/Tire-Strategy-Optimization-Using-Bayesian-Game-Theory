REAL-TIME BAYESIAN STRATEGY
STEP 2 - WEATHER PROBABILITY ESTIMATION
======================================================================

Purpose:
Estimate the historical Dry/Wet probability from the 2025 dataset and create probability scenarios for Bayesian strategy decision-making.

Selected Teams:
- Red Bull Racing
- McLaren
- Ferrari
- Mercedes

Historical Weather Probability:
P(Dry) = 0.981363
P(Wet) = 0.018637

Probability Model:
P(Dry) + P(Wet) = 1

Scenario Range:
Wet probability is varied from 0% to 100% in 5% increments.

Important Interpretation:
The historical probability represents the empirical weather distribution in the selected 2025 data. The scenario probabilities are used for decision sensitivity analysis and do not represent a live weather forecast.
